01 — The spectrum of human control

Autonomy is not a switch.
It is a question of when the human stops mattering.

Every system in this dataset can select or complete an engagement without a human approving that specific act. What separates them is where in the kill chain human judgement drops out — and whether anyone retains a real opportunity to intervene.

The B1 class was not anticipated by the project's original criteria — a human selects the target, but the system completes the engagement unaided, including after link loss. It is the largest and fastest-growing class here. Excluding it entirely would remove roughly a third of the dataset and most of the current combat evidence.

04 — Methodology & limits

What this dataset does and does not claim.

Inclusion

A system qualifies only if it is capable of kinetic force, can select a target from the environment after activation, can initiate or complete engagement without further human approval, exists in an operationally relevant configuration, and is evidenced as fielded.

Directed energy, electronic warfare and cyber are excluded by scope decision. Retired systems are excluded; systems retired by the originator but in service elsewhere are coded to the current operator.

Capability, not doctrine

What is coded is what a system can do. Doctrinal restriction is recorded separately, so the dataset tracks technology rather than policy — a state's rules of engagement can change without notice, and rarely appear in open sources.

Known limitations

  • Manufacturer claims are inflated. Where a vendor claim and an independent assessment conflict, the entry is coded Contested rather than averaged.
  • Combat evidence is asymmetric. Systems used in Ukraine and the Middle East are far better evidenced than Chinese, Iranian and North Korean equivalents. Absence of confirmed effects for PLA systems reflects absence of employment, not absence of capability.
  • Variant granularity is approximate. Loitering munition families iterate faster than open sources track.
  • Confidence lives at claim level. The overall rating shown on each system is a summary judgement; fielding evidence and autonomy evidence frequently diverge.
